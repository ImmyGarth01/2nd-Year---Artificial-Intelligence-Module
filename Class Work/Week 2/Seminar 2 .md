### Definitions ###
##### Intelligence - Referring to the ability to make sound decsions and thoughts whilst taking in the complete context of the situation #####
##### Artificial Intelligence - A computer taking on the ability to make sound decisions, understanding the context #####
##### Agent - the entity that completes actions based on instructions written by either you or by itself (through machine learning)
##### Rationality - The ability to make the most logical decision for the best outcome avoidant of emotion interference #####

### The Turing Test ###
The Turing test is one that tests how humanlike a computer is, a computer can pass the turing test if a human invigilator cannot detect whether the action/decision was made by a human or computer

### Reflex Actions - Are they rational/intelligent?###
Yes, Not every rational decision has to be long and drawn out, in fact if you were to touch a hot plate your reflex to pull away quickly is rational because it means that you won't cause yourself further harm and it is intelligent because it can take into account that the context of the situation is that doing that will hurt you and there is a better alternative to hurting yourself 


### Introspection - Can this be inaccurate? ###
Yes, as humans we are fundamentally flawed and although we are capable of rationality and logic it is often the case that we are guided by emotion instead, this means that even if we look back at our past thoughts, our comments and opinions about them can still be biased. This is why we work so well with others when we need things such as critisim (only if you can take it) because we cant always look objectively at ourselves the way we could look at others
